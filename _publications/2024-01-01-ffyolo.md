Abstract Summary
======
This paper proposes <strong>FFYOLO</strong>, a lightweight forest fire detection model built on YOLOv8 for deployment scenarios where fast and accurate fire detection is required. The method improves YOLOv8 with attention and feature-fusion components to strengthen fire-region representation, reduce missed detections in complex scenes, and keep the model compact for practical monitoring applications.
