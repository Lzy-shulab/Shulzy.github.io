Abstract Summary
======
This paper improves YOLOv8 for unmanned aerial vehicle forest fire detection, where images often contain small fire regions, changing viewpoints, and complex natural backgrounds. The model enhances multi-scale feature representation and detection robustness so that fire targets can be recognized more reliably from UAV imagery.
