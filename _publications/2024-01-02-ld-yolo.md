Abstract Summary
======
This paper presents <strong>LD-YOLO</strong>, a lightweight dynamic detection framework for forest fire and smoke. The model targets the limitations of conventional detectors in small-target, smoke-like, and complex background scenes by improving feature extraction, upsampling, and spatial-context modeling while maintaining efficient inference for practical monitoring systems.
