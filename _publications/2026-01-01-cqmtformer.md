Abstract Summary
======
This manuscript proposes <strong>CQMTformer</strong>, a channel-query enhanced Mamba-Transformer fusion architecture for multivariate time series forecasting. The method is designed to capture cross-channel dependencies and long-range temporal patterns by combining channel-query modeling with Mamba-style sequence modeling and Transformer-based representation learning.
